What's new in Puerto Bravo: Law of the Asphalt

What changes in each version, from the newest to the oldest.

Optional local-game cheats (proposed contribution)

Options, Cheats contains independent toggles, changed with Left and Right; F1 reads their help. All new options are off by default. They are saved with your options and stay set across New Game Plus. New cheats do not apply on online servers.

  • Collect all loose items instantly: Includes briefcases, collectibles and mission items on the current map. Enable unlimited backpack slots too to collect valuables without a space limit.
  • Kill enemies on contact: On foot, defeats hostile enemies within two metres. Leaves civilians and allies alone.
  • Keep inventory when arrested: Keeps weapons, ammunition, armour, merchandise and backpack items. Fines and sentences still apply.
  • Study again immediately: Repeat skill courses and qualification sessions as soon as they finish. Normal fees and session time apply.
  • Academies ignore community standing: Academies ignore prestige, criminal records, wanted status, bans and opening hours. Prerequisite qualifications still apply.
  • Grow base stats with every kill: Each kill earns two damage points, one maximum-health point and 60 practice points per skill. Saved and carried into New Game Plus. Switching off suspends the bonuses.
  • U: teleport instead of walking: U goes to the enemy selected with Q, or otherwise the GPS destination. On foot; vehicles keep their usual controls.
  • Lethal vehicle impacts on enemies and police: A driving impact defeats enemies and police. Allies remain protected.
  • Allow criminal-record expungement at police stations: Adds a voluntary police-station action. Never clears records automatically or erases lifetime statistics.
  • New Game Plus: keep studies and business upgrades: Keeps qualifications, study progress and business upgrades. Options and earned growth always carry over.
  • GPS autopilot for water vehicles: With GPS autopilot enabled using Y, navigates to the destination around islands. Arrow keys and Space override it.
  • GPS autopilot for air vehicles: With Y and a GPS destination, takes off, flies and stops on arrival. Lands when safe; planes require a runway.
  • Fly any vehicle you enter: Ordinary vehicles use flight controls while occupied. Their original model is restored on exit; switching off in flight starts a safe descent.
  • Empty vehicles pursue and ram enemies: Nearby empty vehicles autonomously pursue hostile enemies. Excludes occupied and mission vehicles. Switching off stops them.
  • Infinite ammunition for acquired firearms: Fire owned guns without spending stored rounds, even at zero. Grants no weapons. Collect up to ten million rounds per weapon, counting magazine and reserve.
  • Infinite acquired throwables and rockets: Owned grenades, petrol bombs, rockets and other explosives are not consumed. Collect up to ten million units per weapon; enable surplus conversion to receive money at the ceiling.
  • Unlimited backpack slots: Item and merchandise capacity grows with actual contents, without allocating a huge list or storing infinity. Switching off keeps your items and restores normal limits for further additions.
  • Business upgrades ignore community standing: All business upgrades ignore community prestige. Required qualifications, normal costs and the maximum level still apply.
  • F: enter any selected vehicle: F enters the live vehicle selected with Q or marked in the GPS for U, regardless of distance, locks, occupants, speed or airborne state. Never revives destroyed vehicles. Transfers directly from your current vehicle or passenger seat; without a different selected vehicle, F keeps its normal exit and landing controls.
  • Return incoming ranged shots: Ranged shots aimed at you or your occupied vehicle return to their shooter, even if they would miss. Includes enemy firearm and explosive weapons. Melee, hazards and your own explosions remain unchanged.
  • Convert surplus ammunition pickups into money: Up to ten million rounds per weapon, counting magazine and reserve. Pays surplus ground rounds multiplied by the listed ammunition purchase price, or one euro each if no price is available. Never pays for purchases, rewards or storage transfers.
  • Infinite focus for slow time: Keeps the focus meter at one hundred percent. Backspace toggles slow time, or opens the world speed control when that cheat is enabled. Switching off restores normal drain and recharge. Disabled with connected friends and on servers.
  • World speed control; normal player speed: Backspace opens the world speed control, from 0 percent (stopped) to 500 percent (five times); 100 is normal. Your movement, vehicles and firing keep their speed. Dialogue and menus keep normal timing. Initial value: 25 percent. Switching off restores Backspace focus controls. Offline only.

The existing No damage option now also repairs vehicles as you enter and protects them, including tyres and fire, until you leave. Other vehicles are not protected. Collision detection is kept, so doors, combat, pickups and driving continue to work normally.

Planned heists with your gang

Real flying

  • Helicopters and light planes have altitude. Up Arrow takes off, D climbs, A descends and F lands: the aircraft comes down by itself and, when it touches the ground, you get out. A note sounds when the altitude changes, higher pitched the higher you are.
  • Flying high, the police on the ground lose sight of you. Flying low, everything is as always.
  • The light plane needs the runway of an airport or an airfield, and builds up speed to take off. Anywhere else it is a forced landing, which you have to confirm and which damages it.
  • Aircraft burn fuel, which is refilled at heliports, airports and airfields. The game warns you when you are on reserve; if it runs out, the engine stops and you glide.
  • You go from one town to another on a real crossing: when you fly to the edge of the town, or with Enter at a heliport or an airport, you choose a destination. There is a heading, altitude, different wind on each flight and a control tower that talks to you over the radio.
  • The autopilot does everything if you want, and the crossing can be sped up with N. Enter says how far is left, the heading, the altitude, the wind and the fuel.
  • Things happen in the air: turbulence, another aircraft you have to give way to, and storms you can go around, fly over or go through. With rain there is more wind and more storms.
  • Flying by hand, two clicks tell you which way to turn. When you arrive, the tower gives you the approach; with assisted landing the aircraft descends and lines up on its own, and without it you do it yourself.
  • Two ways to arrive: land at the airfield, or fly in low over the town and set down wherever you want.
  • Hangars at heliports, airports and airfields: they are rented by the day or bought. Aircraft kept there are not lost, they are refueled and repaired there, and they appear on the phone, in My vehicles, from where they can also be sent to the hangar.
  • Hijacking an airliner, with a weapon, at any airfield. It is a major crime: high wanted level, a serious criminal record and a radar that follows you while you fly high. If you land at an airfield with the radar on you, they are waiting for you. Flying low, it loses you.
  • A different job every day at each airfield: someone pays for the hijacked aircraft, delivered to a discreet spot in another town and without the radar on you.
  • The honest road: the commercial pilot license, which you get at the academy, opens up passenger and cargo flights between towns. Three jobs a day at each airfield, with your own aircraft or one of the company's.
  • Explained inside the game, in How flying works, in the menu of any heliport or airport.
  • On an online server you fly the same way and there are hangars; there are no hijackings or commercial flights.
  • With real flight turned off in Options, everything is as before.
