PUERTO BRAVO: LEY DEL ASFALTO
An open-world audio game, in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Polish, and fully accessible. Version 1.7.7.
You play it by ear, with the keyboard or with an Xbox or PlayStation controller.

TO PLAY
  Run PuertoBravo.exe. There is nothing to install.
  Use headphones and have your screen reader open (NVDA or JAWS).
  If there is no screen reader, the game speaks with the Windows voice.
  Inside the game, F1 tells you the keys and the controller buttons at any moment.

THE MANUAL
  It is in the data\docs folder and also opens from the game's main menu:
    manual_en.html            The full manual, navigable by headings.
    teclas_en.html            Every key and every controller button.
    novedades_en.html         What changes in each version.
    cambiar_sonidos_en.html   How to change the sounds and the music.
    editor_mods_en.html       The mod editor's manual.
  Each guide is also in Spanish: the ones ending in _es.

THE LANGUAGE
  The game follows the Windows language: Spanish if it is any variant of Spanish, and
  otherwise English. You can choose it in Options, on the first line, Language. It is also in Vietnamese.

TO PLAY WITH FRIENDS
  Inside the game, open the phone with M and go to Play with friends. Your own game opens
  the session, with or without a password, and you give the address to your friends.
  It is explained in the manual, in the section Playing with friends.

TO UPDATE
  In the game, Options, Updates. You can choose where it updates from.
  In Options, Updates, Components, you will find the languages, voices, guides, sound packs
  and mods that are published without waiting for a new version.
  Your saved games, your options, your music and your mods are not touched.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  The game writes down what happens in each session in the data\logs folder, one folder per
  session, with the date and time in its name. It keeps the last five. It has no personal data.
  Compress into a ZIP the folder of the session where it failed and send it, saying what you
  were doing:
    https://github.com/hxebolax/PuertoBravo/issues
    or on Mastodon: https://comunidad.nvda.es/@HXeBoLaX
  While playing, F12 puts a mark in the log at the moment you notice something odd.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, OPTIONAL
  People in the street can answer with an artificial intelligence. Turn it on in Options,
  People and artificial intelligence. From there you can also download a free one that
  works without the internet. Google AI Studio, OpenAI, Anthropic and other services work too.

TO MAKE MODS
  Run EditorMods.exe. It is an editor accessible with a screen reader.
  F1 opens its manual and Control H explains each field.

YOUR THINGS
  data\partidas           Your saved games.
  data\config             Your options.
  data\radio\mi_musica    Your music, for the My music station.
  data\mods               The mods.
  If you pass the game on to someone else, don't give them data\partidas or data\config.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 or 11 and stereo headphones.
